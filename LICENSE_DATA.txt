id-generator's data, in resources/, comes from these sources. See doc/data.md
for how each table is built.

Derived by the scripts in scripts/:

places.tsv, place_cities.tsv
  - France: INSEE, Code officiel geographique 2025 (communes, arrondissements,
    communes since 1943, countries). Licence Ouverte / Open Licence 2.0
    (https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence), which requires
    credit to INSEE.
  - Italy: Ministero dell'Interno, ANPR, Archivio comuni and table 2, Stati
    esteri (https://www.anagrafenazionale.interno.it/area-tecnica/tabelle-di-decodifica/),
    from Istat, the Agenzia delle Entrate and the foreign ministry. The page
    states no licence. Only official codes and names are used.
  - China, Indonesia, Thailand: Wikidata (properties P13971, P2588, P300, P1566,
    P1082). CC0 1.0.
  - The United States: Social Security Administration, area numbers by state.
    US public domain.
  - Cities and the population weights: GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/),
    through city-generator. Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
    (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), which requires
    credit to GeoNames.

series.tsv
  - The United States: Social Security Administration, High Group Lists from
    1999 to 2011, as archived by the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine.
    US public domain.

test_numbers.tsv
  - Sweden: Skatteverket, test personnummer, published as open data. The
    metadata read states no licence.
  - Iceland: Thjodskra, the six test numbers on its page about kennitala.

countries.tsv
  - The population column is copied from country-generator, which states its
    own sources. The ICAO codes follow ICAO Doc 9303 Part 3.

Curated for this library from laws, the authorities' published rules, reference
works and specimens: the facts in schemes.tsv, documents.tsv, validity.tsv,
name_rules.tsv, the other rows of places.tsv and series.tsv, and the primary
scheme in countries.tsv. Each scheme and document row is based on the sources
named in scripts/curated/survey_numbers.tsv and survey_documents.tsv, among them
Microsoft Purview's sensitive information type definitions (CC BY 4.0, credit
to Microsoft) and Wikipedia and Wikidata for facts. No text or images are
shipped from them, and specimen images were only read. These facts are released
under the MIT licence of the library.
